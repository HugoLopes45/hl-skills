# Notes: late invoice reminders

Topic: how freelance designers chase a late invoice without losing the client.
Audience: freelance graphic designers who invoice small businesses.
Goal: a reader copies a reminder email and sends it on day 14 after the due date.
Market and language: United States, English (stated by the requester).
Research: three calls with designers in March. Two said they chase on day 14 after the due date. Source: call notes, March.
