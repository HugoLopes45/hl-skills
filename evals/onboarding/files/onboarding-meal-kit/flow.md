# Flow: meal-kit app, first week

Goal stated by the team: new users choose a first box.
Steps:
1. Sign up with email.
2. Pick diet (vegetarian, omnivore, or none).
3. Pick household size (1, 2, 4).
4. Pick two recipes from a list of 12.
5. Enter delivery address and slot.
6. Pay for the first box.
Recorded events: signup_completed, box_paid.
Not recorded: recipe selection, delivery slot chosen.
