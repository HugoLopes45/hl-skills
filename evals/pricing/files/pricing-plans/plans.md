# Loomwell plans

Supplied by the owner, 2026-10-02. Product: a pattern-sharing app for independent sewists. Designers publish patterns; sewists buy downloads.

Current plans (as shown on the pricing page, 2026-10-01):
- Free: 2 published patterns, ads shown.
- Maker: 7 euros per month, billed monthly; unlimited patterns; no ads. Prices exclude VAT.
- Maker annual: 70 euros per year.

Cost to serve: hosting costs about 0.04 euros per download. This is the owner's estimate from the last invoice; it has not been measured per download.

Buyer evidence: a survey of 8 responses, sent 2026-09-12. Question wording: "What is the most you would pay per month for Maker?" Method: one question in an online form, no screening, responses kept as received. Columns in survey.csv: respondent_id, role, max_monthly_eur (the answer to the question above, in euros).

Owner's plan: raise Maker to 9 euros for all users next month. Reason given: the team thinks other pattern apps charge more. No competitor price check was done.
