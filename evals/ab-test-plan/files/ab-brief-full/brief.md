# Brief: bike checkout button

Site: bike rental booking site.
Page: checkout, step 3 of 3.
Change: the button label moves from "Pay" to "Confirm booking".
Owner: growth team.
Baseline: 62.0% of checkout visits complete a booking (export from 1 to 28 April, owner-supplied).
Minimum effect: 2.0 percentage points, the smallest lift the owner would act on.
Alpha: 0.05 (owner accepted).
Power: 0.80 (owner accepted).
Unit: visitor, first checkout visit in the test period.
Traffic: 1,800 checkout visits per week (export, same period).
