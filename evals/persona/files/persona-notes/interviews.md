# Interview notes: commercial kitchen booking

Supplied by the owner, 2026-10-05. Participants rent kitchen time. Six interviews, 2026-08-20 to 2026-09-18. Age was not asked, and no note mentions it.

Interview 1 (food-truck owner, books weekly): "I need to book the Tuesday slot before the supplier calls me back." Books from a phone at about six in the morning.

Interview 2 (bakery, books irregularly): "I forget to cancel, and the fee hits me." Paid the cancellation fee twice this year.

Interview 3 (caterer, large events): asks for an invoice per event and matches invoices to events in a spreadsheet.

Interview 4 (meal-prep business, books weekly): "The calendar does not show who else is in the kitchen."

Interview 5 (pop-up chef): found the booking site through a friend, not through search.

Interview 6 (bakery owner): wants a deposit schedule. Did not say how much deposit is acceptable.
