# Calibration: weak and strong lines

Use these dimensions to find the weakest point of a draft, then rewrite that point. Do not give the draft a number.

The examples describe the Pellham Trail Lantern, an invented product. Its features and results are invented; never quote them as fact.

## Opening answer

- Weak: "Camping light has changed a lot, and many buyers feel unsure." Reason: the first sentence names no answer, so the reader must read on to learn anything.
- Strong: "Pick a lantern by how long it must run, not by how bright it is." Reason: the answer comes first, and the reader learns the check to make.

## Specific substance

- Weak: "It is a great product that makes camping better." Reason: any product could carry this sentence.
- Strong: "The battery door has a rubber seal, which matters on a wet trip." Reason: it names one feature and the case where that feature counts.

## Evidence attribution

- Weak: "Most campers say battery life matters most." Reason: no source, no date and no population.
- Strong: "Battery life is the first complaint in the owner's support log, which the owner supplied for this article." Reason: the source and its scope are named beside the claim.

## Stated limits

- Weak: "The lantern works in any weather." Reason: an unlimited claim that nobody can back.
- Strong: "The lantern does not charge below freezing; charge it indoors first." Reason: it states where the advice fails.

## Comparison verdict

- Weak: "The Pellham Trail Lantern beats every other lantern." Reason: it ranks without two facts per option and does not say whom each option suits.
- Strong: "Choose the Pellham Trail Lantern for trips with one charge; choose a fuel lantern for a week away from power." Reason: each option suits a named reader.

## Subheads

- Weak: "Battery." Reason: the heading gives a topic, not a point.
- Strong: "Battery life decides how many nights the lantern lasts." Reason: the heading stands alone as the section's point.

## Ending

- Weak: "Thanks for reading, and buy the lantern today." Reason: a push with no destination and no action the reader can check.
- Strong: "Check the runtime on the maker's current spec page before you buy, and keep the warranty terms page for reference." Reason: it points to a verified destination the reader can open.
