# Newsletter edition

Use this file when the request asks for a recurring newsletter edition. The article rules in `SKILL.md` and `article-method.md` apply to every item and paragraph.

## Shape

- Choose the shape from the reader's job: a catch-up on the week, one idea taught in depth, a personal view, a curated set of links, a daily briefing, or one original idea with a short link section.
- Use the shape the user names. Without one, pick from the job and state the choice in the Sources and assumptions note.
- Keep the recurring section names and their order stable. Vary the sentences: the opening move of the draft must differ from the opening move of the last two supplied editions.

## Subject and opening

- Write three subject lines that differ in mechanism: a specific curiosity, a question the reader already asks, or a claim the body supports.
- Do not reuse a prior subject's structure and topic together. Check each new subject against the prior subjects the user supplies. Do not reuse one structure on an adjacent topic in two sends in a row.
- Pay the subject off in the first paragraph. The preheader extends the subject and never repeats it.
- Open with the most interesting true thing in the edition. No greeting and no edition preamble.

## Items

- Cite only pages read in this session, primary sources first: the paper, the changelog or the original report, not a summary of it. Date each item; corroborate an undated item before including it.
- Give each item a comment that adds what the source does not say. A link with a restated headline is a feed, not curation.
- Keep an item only when the reader would forward that one link to a peer in the niche. Cut "fine but not great" items.
- Mark a paywalled source as "(paywalled)".

## Voice and closing action

- Use the voice the user supplies. Without one, write plain, specific prose and say so in the note.
- Use no stock openers, greetings or sign-offs unless the supplied voice has them.
- Give the edition one closing action. Other asks are passing mentions at most.
- Bold only the one clause per section that matters most. Bold on every line flags nothing.

## Judging a send

- Use send history only when the user supplies it.
- Judge a subject line on clicks. Treat open counts as a weaker signal, a direction only.

## Output

Use the artifact fence from `SKILL.md`: the chosen subject is the Title, the preheader is the Summary, and the section list is the Outline. List the two other subject lines in the Sources and assumptions note. Before handover, check that each link was read in this session and that the edition has one closing action.

Only when the user names a mail app: before handover, check its current official source for a clipping limit; if the edition is above it, shorten the edition or link the rest. If the source cannot be read, mark this check unverified in the Sources and assumptions note.
