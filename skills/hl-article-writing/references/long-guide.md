# Long guide

Use this file when the request asks for a long guide: one multi-part piece that a reader saves and acts on. The article rules in `SKILL.md` and `article-method.md` apply to every part.

## Parts

Use only the parts the brief and sources support, in this order. Each part used is one Body section; the Outline lists them.

1. The answer in one sentence.
2. The size of the gap: how big the problem is, with sourced figures.
3. The whole idea in short numbered steps the reader can act on.
4. How we got here: the few stages that explain the problem.
5. Why this audience: who has the problem and how they describe it.
6. What to offer: what the offer does, and what it does not do.
7. The money math, labeled as `article-method.md` requires.
8. How to do it: copyable prompts or steps, as `article-method.md` requires.
9. Keeping it working: what the reader does after the first run.
10. What breaks: where the advice fails, as `article-method.md` requires.
11. Start this week: one action the reader takes this week.
12. The close: answer the reader's question and point to a destination the author owns, as `article-method.md` requires.

## Read time

State the read time in the opening, after the answer: word count divided by a reading speed, rounded. Use 240 words per minute as a heuristic default, and say so in the Sources and assumptions note.
