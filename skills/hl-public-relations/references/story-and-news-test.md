# Story and News Test

## News test

A story fails when any check fails. Name each check's source in the plan; a check the input does not settle is `unknown`, not a fail.

1. **Current to the reader.** The event happened inside the window the owner sets (`Default: none`; an unset window is `unknown`).
2. **Useful to the outlet's readers.** Name the reader and the fact, decision aid, or view they gain.
3. **Backed by proof.** A document, a dataset, a result, or a customer who agreed to be named. Without proof, this check fails.
4. **Speakable.** A person who approved the wording can explain it on the record.

A product launch alone usually fails check 2. A milestone passes only with a narrative: what changed, for whom, and why it matters.

## Angles

Pick one angle per pitch.

- **Founding story:** why the founder started and what the team learned, from supplied facts. Use it when the founder can speak on the record.
- **Underdog:** a smaller team does one named thing better than a larger rival. Name the size gap and the thing only from supplied facts.
- **Broken system:** a practice in the field that the company opposes on the record. The enemy is the practice, never a named competitor; an attack on a rival reads as a smear.
- **Data story:** one surprising number from proprietary data, with its method and sample size. Without them, the outlet cannot check the number.

The owner chooses the angle after checking the proof.

## Reactive comment

Skip without drafting when:

- The story is about a death, an injury, a disaster, or another tragedy.
- The story is politically charged and the owner has not supplied a brand position on it.
- The company has no real expertise or data on the topic.
- The owner judges the news window closed. A late comment has no reader.
- The only angle is the product. That is marketing, not a comment.

Each check must pass before drafting:

- **Timely:** the comment can reach the reporter while the story is still in the news.
- **Relevant:** the company has expertise or data on the topic.
- **Unique:** the comment adds a point that the coverage the owner supplied does not make.
- **Backed:** a supplied fact, dataset, or customer supports it.
- **Held:** the owner holds the position and will be quoted on it.

Reaction angles:

- **Data response:** supplied data adds to or contradicts the story.
- **Customer impact:** a customer the owner can name, with approval.
- **Explainer:** the story lacks a point the company can state from experience.
- **Contrarian:** only with backing from the checks above.

A comment that says only "we are watching this" gives a reporter no quote. Do not write one.
