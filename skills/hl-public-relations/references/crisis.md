# Crisis

## Severity tiers

| Tier | Example | Who approves |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Contained | one feature bug, one complaint thread | support lead replies; no public statement |
| 2. Visible | service slowdown, a complaint gaining attention | support or marketing lead signs a public note |
| 3. Significant | full outage, billing error across many customers | executive approves; one spokesperson |
| 4. Severe | data breach, safety issue, executive misconduct, regulator inquiry | adviser clears any statement before publication |

When unsure, assign the higher tier.

## First hour

1. Confirm the facts with the people who know them directly, not from summaries.
2. Assign the tier. For tier 4, send the question to the adviser at once.
3. Name one spokesperson and one channel of record.
4. Publish a holding statement.
5. Give staff the same words, and route press and customer questions to one person.
6. Set the next update time, publish it, and keep it.

Tier 1 runs steps 1 to 3 only. Tiers 2 to 4 run all six.

## Channel order

1. Directly affected people, by direct message.
2. Staff, in parallel with step 1.
3. The status or incident page, as the source of truth.
4. Owned social channels, linking to the statement.
5. Press, answered after steps 1 to 4. A reporter's question does not change this order.

## Holding statement

Use this shape for a holding statement and for each update before the facts are confirmed. It has these three parts and no others:

```text
We are aware of [confirmed issue, in plain words].
[PLACEHOLDER: confirmed actions, or an owner-approved commitment; state an investigation only if confirmed].
Our next update is at [PLACEHOLDER: time and time zone, set by the owner].
```

Rules:

- Confirmed facts only. Leave out cause, scope, and affected groups until they are confirmed.
- No denial, no admission of fault, and no "we believe".
- No apology unless the owner and adviser approve its exact words.
- No legal wording.
- Give a count only when it is confirmed, with its source tag.

Outage and billing-error updates use the same shape.

## Full statement

Write a full statement once the owner confirms cause, scope, and affected groups. It states who is affected, the confirmed facts, the actions taken, and the time of the next update. The rules above still apply.

## Outside this skill

List these as questions for the owner's adviser. Do not answer them:

- Notification duties and deadlines for a breach or another incident.
- Whether to admit fault or liability.
- Refund, credit, or compensation terms.
- Contact with a regulator.
- Employment matters.
- Sponsored-content disclosure rules in each country.

The owner and team write any postmortem after the root cause is confirmed.
