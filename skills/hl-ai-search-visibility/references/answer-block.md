# Answer block calibration

Read when judging whether an answer block can be lifted, or when the page is a health, legal, or financial page (workflow step 4). The product, Brightcase, is invented, and so are its figures and quotes. Judge each strong line by its reason, not by its wording.

## Direct answer

- Weak: "Brightcase is a tool many freelancers find useful for billing."
  Reason: it does not answer the question the heading asks, so no passage can be lifted as the answer.
- Strong: "Brightcase sends an invoice with a payment link, then a reminder at 7 and 14 days after the due date."
  Reason: the first sentence answers the question on its own, with the specific mechanism.

## Entity named in full

- Weak: "It sends reminders automatically."
  Reason: "it" does not survive being lifted out of the page.
- Strong: "Brightcase sends reminders automatically."
  Reason: the answer names the product, so it stays attributable when quoted alone.

## Number with unit, date and source

- Weak: "Late payment costs freelancers 20."
  Reason: no unit of measure, date or source, so a reader or an answer engine cannot check it.
- Strong: "In Brightcase's own sample of 1,200 invoices from January to June 2026, 14% were paid after the due date."
  Reason: the number has its sample, period and origin, so the claim can be checked.

## Quote from a named role

- Weak: "Our product is the best."
  Reason: a claim without an accountable source adds nothing a reader can verify.
- Strong: "Late invoices come from unclear due dates, not unwilling clients," says the operations lead at Brightcase.
  Reason: a role attributes the statement without a personal name, and the reader knows who said it.

## Domain pages

- Health: name the author's credential and the date a qualified reviewer last checked the text, when the owner can show both.
- Legal or financial: check that the page names the jurisdiction or regulator; put the disclaimer question to the owner's adviser as a Next step.

Use these only when the page's topic falls in that domain.
