# Ambition lens

Raise the target, not its size. An outside judge decides whether the raise worked.

## Principles

- Better, not bigger. Cut any raise that adds work without improving the result.
- Aim at the outcome beyond the task: fix every crash of this kind, not this one.
- Work like the field's best: their raw data, methods, and tools, not their vocabulary.
- Bring in the fields the result depends on, for what they do, not how they talk.
- Every raise has an outside judge: a test, a benchmark, held-out data, a measurement. No judge: step down.
- Name missing access and what it unlocks. Never invent a source, tool, or result.
- Ambition raises the standard, not the permissions: replacing the request, spending, or irreversible steps are user decisions.

## Modes

### `raise` (default)

1. **Goal beyond the goal**: one line.
2. **Ladder**: 3 rungs, each higher, each with its judge.
3. **Access**: what each rung needs, present (checked) or missing.
4. **Rewritten prompt**: one self-contained fence, ending with the judge and what counts as a pass.
5. **Verdict**: `Raised` / `Needs access` / `Already ambitious`.

## Stand down

Throwaway work, an emergency fix wanted minimal now, or a request for the smallest version (use `minimalist`).
