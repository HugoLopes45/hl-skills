# Carousel method

Use this file for a carousel or document post. Pick the structure, then write slide 1, the middle slides and the close. Read [post-method.md](post-method.md) too: its evidence, hook and capture rules still apply.

## Choose one structure

Pick one structure from the content. Write the slide plan after the choice.

| Content | Structure |
|---|---|
| A list of tools, tips or resources | Count list |
| A result the user reached with a method | Result then method |
| Several named techniques on one theme | Numbered techniques |
| A firm opinion on a common practice | Opinion with a fair limit |
| A product or workflow the user can show | Walkthrough |

- **Count list.** Slide 1 states the exact count and the deliverable. Each middle slide gives one item with its sub-items. The last slide asks for one action.
- **Result then method.** Slide 1 states the supplied result with its source line. Slide 2 names the problem. Middle slides name the tools and steps. The last slide shows the output as a real capture.
- **Numbered techniques.** Slide 1 states a checkable claim about a common mistake. Slide 2 says why the usual approach fails. Each middle slide gives one named technique. The last slide states one thesis and one action.
- **Opinion with a fair limit.** Slide 1 states the opinion. Middle slides give specific, supplied evidence. The second-last slide says what the author does not object to, and why. The last slide ends on a supplied fact.
- **Walkthrough.** Slide 1 shows the finished result. Slide 2 names the problem it solves. Slide 3 lists the steps in order. One slide per step follows, each with a real capture. The last slide shows the full output.

## Rules

- Slide 1 works alone. Test it as a single feed post: a reader must understand it and want the next slide without the others.
- One idea per slide. Split a slide that needs two sentences of set-up.
- Use one visual template on every interior slide: same layout, type scale and colors.
- A count on the cover equals the slides that deliver it. Cut filler slides rather than pad the count.
- Name each technique and step. A vague middle slide makes the proof look like luck.
- Use real captures for product steps, under the default image rule in [post-method.md](post-method.md). Do not draw a mock screen.
- Apply the platform spec step in [post-method.md](post-method.md) to slide count, slide size and aspect ratio.
- The last slide carries at most one call to action and does not restate earlier slides, except that a walkthrough's last slide shows the full output.
- The caption states a different fact from slide 1. Do not repeat slide 1.

## Output

Put the slides in a `markdown` fence, one heading per slide in order. Put the caption under its own heading after the last slide. Give each image alt text, as [post-method.md](post-method.md) requires.
