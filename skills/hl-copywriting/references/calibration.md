# Calibration

Use this file to judge copy before it ships, or to set the target for a rewrite. Score each dimension as weak, adequate, or strong, then rewrite the weakest dimension first. For each weak score, quote the line that caused it; a score with no line attached cannot be acted on. Scores are working notes and never appear in the deliverable.

Every example uses an invented mail-in knife sharpening service. Every number and detail in the examples is invented. The contrast shows craft, not facts.

## Dimensions

- **Clarity:** Does a distracted reader get the core message in one pass? Read it as a reader who does not know the product; list each term they cannot repeat, and replace it.
- **Specificity:** Are there real numbers, named details, or a concrete mechanism?
- **Voice:** Does one identifiable person speak, not a committee?
- **Desire:** Is the after-state a scene the reader can picture, not an adjective? Mark it not applicable when the brief supplies no after-state, then judge the remaining dimensions.
- **Proof:** Is every claim backed by supplied evidence? Mark it not applicable when no proof exists, then judge the remaining dimensions. Skeptical pass: list each unsupported claim a distrustful buyer would question near the call to action; each is left out, or becomes a placeholder only in the two cases under "Placeholders are rare" in SKILL.md.
- **Urgency:** Is there an honest reason to act now? A stated cost of waiting is enough; frame it as what the reader loses by waiting. Never invent scarcity. Mark it not applicable when no honest reason exists, then judge the remaining dimensions.
- **Flow:** Does each line pull to the next, from headline to call to action?
- **Originality:** Could only this product run this piece? Underline what comes from this brief and no other; a paragraph with nothing underlined is weak.
- **Tone fit:** Does the register match the moment and the voice evidence? Humor on a blocking error is weak; so is a flat line on a 404 page for a brand whose buyers call it charming.

## Anchors

**Clarity**

- Weak: "This service is a sharpening solution that empowers home cooks to maintain their blades." Reason: the reader cannot say what the service does.
- Strong: "Mail us a dull knife. We sharpen it and post it back in 5 working days." (invented) Reason: a reader can repeat it to a friend.

**Specificity**

- Weak: "Thousands of customers trust us to keep their knives in top shape." Reason: "thousands" and "top shape" fit any sharpener.
- Strong: "Each knife is ground to the angle on its label, then checked under a 10x loupe." (invented) Reason: the method is concrete and checkable.

**Voice**

- Weak: "At our shop, we believe every cook deserves excellent tools." Reason: a committee writes this, and any brand can use it.
- Strong: "I sharpened my first knife in my mother's garage. It took me a weekend and a bag of stones." (invented) Reason: one person, one specific detail.

**Desire**

- Weak: "Enjoy the convenience of a sharp kitchen." Reason: "convenience" asks the reader to imagine the result.
- Strong: "Sunday, the onions slide off the blade. [after-state the brief supplies, in the buyer's words]" (invented scene) Reason: a moment the reader can stand in; without a supplied after-state, mark Desire not applicable.

**Proof**

- Weak: "The best sharpening service you can trust." Reason: a claim about a claim, with no evidence attached.
- Strong: "Each returned knife ships with a record of its edge-angle check." (invented) Reason: the claim names the evidence the buyer can ask for.

**Urgency**

- Weak: "Only a few spots left. Act now before it is too late!" Reason: invented scarcity, which the reader discounts.
- Strong: "Orders placed before the 14th return before the holiday weekend." (invented) Reason: a dated, honest reason with its cost stated.

**Originality**

- Weak: "Professional knife sharpening you can rely on, delivered to your door." Reason: every sharpener can run it unchanged.
- Strong: "Most of the knives we get were never bad. They were kept in a drawer with the potato masher." (invented) Reason: it is built from what this bench sees.

**Tone fit**

- Weak: "Oops! Your payment took a little tumble." Reason: a joke at a reader who is blocked and may lose access.
- Strong: "Your card was declined, so the order is not placed yet. Your knives have not been charged for. Try another card." (invented) Reason: what happened, what is safe, what to do.
- Weak: "Error 404. The requested resource could not be found." for a brand with a dry, warm voice. Reason: the one spare moment on the site, wasted.
- Strong: "This page is as missing as the edge on a drawer knife. The sharpening form is this way." (invented) Reason: the product's world, then the way out.

**Flow**

- Weak: "We sharpen knives. Our team is skilled. You should try us." Reason: each sentence stops, and nothing pulls to the next.
- Strong: "Knives in a drawer knock against each other and lose their edge. Send yours back, and we regrind it in 5 working days." (invented) Reason: the first sentence sets up the next one.
