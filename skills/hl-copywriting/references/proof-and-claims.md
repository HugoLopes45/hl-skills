# Proof and claims

Use this file before you write any factual line.

## Claim basis

| Basis | Action |
|---|---|
| Supplied in the brief | Write it with its own limits. |
| Read in a checked source, with its link | Write it, and name the link in Next. |
| Neither | Write `[PLACEHOLDER: proof needed]`, naming the proof that replaces it. |
| Not needed for the reader's decision | Omit it. |

## Never write, unless supplied

- Testimonials, customer names, quotes, star ratings, review counts, user counts, logos, "as seen in" lines, awards, or certifications.
- Guarantees, refunds, delivery or setup times, savings, or results.
- Comparisons with named competitors.

## Declined claims name their unlock

When the user asks for an unsupported claim, do not write it and do not drop it silently. Answer with four parts:

1. The requested claim, quoted.
2. Why it falls short: no source supplied, sample not stated, or not measured.
3. The closest supported wording, or a placeholder.
4. The evidence that would unlock the original: a signed customer quote, a rating export with its platform and date, a measured test with its method, or a published policy.

Example: "Requested: '4.8 stars from 12,000 reviews'. No rating export was supplied. Closest supported line: `[PLACEHOLDER: average rating and review count, from the export of your review platform]`. Unlock: that export, with its platform and date."

## Numbers

- Give each number its unit, its period, and its basis: supplied, measured with a stated method, checked source, or estimated from stated assumptions.
- A price from the brief is supplied. A price you derive is estimated; show the assumption beside it.
