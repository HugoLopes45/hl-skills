# Copy Calibration

Use this file to check headlines, one-liners, landing sections, and posts before the output. Check each dimension on its own. A line passes a dimension when its reason matches the strong example, not when its wording does. The examples use AcmeRoute, an invented app that logs delivery-van stops. They are not product facts; never copy them into a real draft.

No score gate applies. Revise a failing line once. If a claim-support or scarcity dimension still fails, replace the unsupported claim or limit with a `[PLACEHOLDER]`, and drop any urgency that has no checkable limit. If a tone or other dimension still fails, ship the best revision and say under line 1, in plain words, what the line still lacks.

## Buyer's words

- Weak: "Streamline your fleet operations with our powerful platform." The words come from the vendor, not the buyer.
- Strong: "Stop retyping stop notes at 9 p.m." The line uses a situation the buyer names in their own reviews or threads. Reason: the reader recognizes the moment.

## Claim support

- Weak: "Cuts paperwork time in half." No supplied figure or method supports the claim.
- Strong: "Logs each stop in one tap. [PLACEHOLDER: measured time saving and how it was measured]." The action is checkable and the figure waits for evidence. Reason: every claim has a source or a placeholder.

## Specificity

- Weak: "Works for every business." Any product could say it, so it names no buyer.
- Strong: "Built for owners of one to five vans who log stops on paper." The line names a buyer and a situation. Reason: a non-target could not claim it unchanged.

## The ask

- Weak: "Learn more about AcmeRoute today!" The reader does not know what the next step gives them.
- Strong: "Get the printable stop sheet. No signup." The action and what the reader gets are both named. Reason: the reader knows the cost and the result before clicking.

## Tone

- Weak: "Revolutionary, game-changing routing!" Hype asserts value without evidence.
- Strong: "Reorders your stops by postcode." The line states the mechanism in plain words. Reason: the reader can judge the claim.

## Scarcity

Apply this dimension only to a line that claims a limit or deadline.

- Weak: "Limited time only: join before the offer ends!" No end date exists, so the reader cannot check the claim.
- Strong: "Onboarding is capped at [PLACEHOLDER: monthly seat limit]; the page shows the seats left." The limit is real, checkable, and enforced when reached. Reason: the reader can verify the constraint. The seat limit is a placeholder in this invented example, not a product fact.
