# Legal and carrier checks for text messages

Read this file when a message depends on a rule for a country, a region, or a sending practice. Each item says what to confirm. This file states no rule: confirm each one in the current official document for the market, and send each adviser question to the owner's adviser.

Starting points to look up in their current official text, and state in your answer the date you checked each: the US Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and the rules of the Federal Communications Commission; the EU General Data Protection Regulation and the national rules under the ePrivacy Directive; the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations; Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

## Consent

- Which consent form the current rule requires for marketing texts in each recipient country, and whether a transactional text needs any consent. Adviser question.
- Whether consent given at checkout covers texts sent later about the order. Adviser question.
- Whether a pre-ticked box, a condition of purchase, or a bundled terms acceptance is allowed. Adviser question.
- Whether an existing contact needs another consent before the first marketing text. Adviser question.

## Sending rules

- The permitted send hours in the recipient's local time, for each country and region, including stricter regional rules. Check the official text for each region; the adviser confirms the window.
- The sender registration or identification the carrier or the national regulator requires before sending from a given number type. Check the current official requirement for the country.
- The content each message must carry: sender identity, any cost notice, the help instruction, and a contact address. Check each market's text.
- Whether a chat app may carry a free-form business message only inside the platform's reply window, and otherwise only an approved template. Check the platform's current official document.
