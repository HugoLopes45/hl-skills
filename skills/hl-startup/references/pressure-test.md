# Pressure Test

Identify the assumption that matters most, observed customer pain, current alternatives, and relevant founder access or experience. Separate supplied facts, researched evidence, and hypotheses. Missing customer or founder evidence stays unknown.

Size the market only when the verdict depends on it: use two independent methods, such as reachable buyers times price and a market figure with a checked source. Show a range, the assumptions apart from facts, and the assumption that moves the answer most. If either method lacks a supplied or checked input, say the size is unknown and name the missing input.

Lead with the supported conclusion in plain words; put proceed, revise, stop, or insufficient evidence on the verdict line. Then rank demonstrated weaknesses by consequence and propose the cheapest test of the decisive unknown. Do not invent fatal flaws, impersonate an investor, or replace uncertainty with a harsh tone.
