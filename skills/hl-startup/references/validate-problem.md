# Validate Problem

State the pain, context, frequency, and current workaround using supplied evidence. Judge the pain by how often it happens and what it costs the buyer; a rare or small pain is a weak case: return `Revise`, not `Proceed`; put the decision to continue in Next. Describe an identifiable early-adopter profile without inventing a real person. Label an unvalidated role or responsibility as a recruitment hypothesis, including inside the interview draft.

Draft five non-leading questions about past behavior, costs, and attempted solutions. Propose observable validation and rejection criteria, labeling thresholds as proposals. Distinguish reported interest from actions such as repeated use or payment.

Open with what the evidence supports and what interviews or experiments must still establish. A research plan does not validate demand or willingness to pay.
