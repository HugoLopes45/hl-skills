# Competition Map

Identify current behavior, including doing nothing when supported, then direct and indirect alternatives. Research with available primary sources; if unavailable, label candidates unverified rather than inventing companies or features.

Open with the strongest alternative and whether the differentiation is supported. Then compare in a table of three short columns: alternative, price (with its source, or "no source"), why a customer would switch or stay; the difference you offer goes in one sentence under it. Customer awareness and satisfaction require evidence; leave them unknown instead of scoring them from intuition.

State why switching might be worthwhile and which assumptions need testing. Report search scope and sources; do not claim every competitor was found.
