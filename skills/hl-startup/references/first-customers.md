# First Customers

Identify supported channels or existing contacts and label unverified candidates. Draft personal outreach asking for a conversation; use visible placeholders for unknown names or details. Do not invent familiarity or send messages.

Propose behavioral success signals and weekly actions toward 10 customers. State access, conversion, and pricing assumptions; the target is not a forecast. Propose no ads, no automation, and no guaranteed acquisition timeline.

Open with the most reachable channel and why. Then channels, outreach approach, message draft, signals, a milestone table (week, action, success signal), and the next testable assumption.
