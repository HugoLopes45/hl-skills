# Pressure Test

Identify the assumption that matters most, observed customer pain, current alternatives, and relevant founder access or experience. Separate supplied facts, researched evidence, and hypotheses. Missing customer or founder evidence stays unknown.

Lead with proceed, revise, stop, or insufficient evidence. Then rank demonstrated weaknesses by consequence and propose the cheapest test of the decisive unknown. Do not invent fatal flaws, impersonate an investor, or replace uncertainty with a harsh tone.
