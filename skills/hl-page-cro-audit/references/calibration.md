# Calibration: judging a line

**Load this when** a headline, proof line, or action label is weak or strong and the finding needs a reason. Use these lines to judge only. Do not paste them into the audited page; name the change instead.

All lines use an invented product, a shift planner for restaurant managers. The lines are invented examples, not measured results.

## Value statement: names the outcome

- Weak: "Smarter scheduling for modern teams." Reason: it names no user, no task and no result. Any scheduling product could use it.
- Strong: "Build next week's rota for your kitchen in one screen." Reason: it names the user (kitchen), the task (rota) and the result (one screen). A visitor can check it against their own week.

## Primary action label: names the action and the result

- Weak: "Get started" Reason: it names no action and no result, so the visitor cannot tell what the first click does.
- Strong: "Build my first rota" Reason: the verb is the action, and the object is the result the visitor gets.
- Weak: "Submit" on a demo request form. Reason: it names the form, not the result, so the visitor cannot tell what they get after the click.
- Strong: "Book my rota walkthrough" Reason: the verb is the action, and the object names what the visitor gets from the form.

## Promise match: the words the visitor came for

- Ad text: "Free rota templates for restaurants."
- Weak headline: "Plan your team's week with less stress." Reason: a paraphrase. A visitor who scans for "free rota templates" does not find them.
- Strong headline: "Free rota templates for restaurants." Reason: the same words as the ad, so the visitor sees the offer they clicked.

## Proof: sourced and near the action

- Weak: "Trusted by 10,000 restaurants." beside the price, with no source on the page or in the supplied material. Reason: the count is unsourced, so it cannot support the price until its source is shown.
- Strong: "Used by 42 venues, per the account export supplied with this audit." beside the button. Reason: the count names its source, which is in the supplied material, and it sits next to the action it supports.

For each layer, name the weakest line and its change in the finding. Do not give the page a score.
